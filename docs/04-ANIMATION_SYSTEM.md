# Animation Philosophy

Everything breathes.
Nothing loops mechanically.
Everything moves slowly.

## Torches
Independent randomized flicker for:
- brightness
- glow radius
- light intensity
- flame sprite

## Fog
Three layers moving at different speeds.

## Tree
Maximum branch movement: 2px.

## Banners
Slow wind with randomized gusts.

## Camera
Breathing zoom: 100% → 100.5% over 30s.

## Clouds
Very slow movement (minutes).

## Mouse
Maximum parallax: 15px.
