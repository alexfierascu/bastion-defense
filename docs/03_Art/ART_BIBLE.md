# Art Bible

Status: Approved

---

# Purpose

Defines the visual identity of Bastion Defense.

This document overrides all artistic decisions made by AI.

If a generated asset does not comply with this document, it must be rejected.

---

# Design Statement

Bastion Defense is not "another fantasy tower defense."

It is the story of the final fortress where civilization survives.

Nature did not destroy the fortress.

Nature embraced it.

The fortress became alive.

Stone and roots became one organism.

This identity must appear everywhere.

Menus.

Gameplay.

Buildings.

Enemies.

UI.

Animations.

Marketing.

Everything.

---

# Emotional Goals

The player should feel:

• Safety

• Solitude

• Wonder

• Ancient history

• Hope

• Quiet before war

Never horror.

Never cartoon.

Never exaggerated fantasy.

# Materials

Only believable materials.

Stone

Old oak

Bronze

Iron

Leather

Canvas

Roots

Moss

Wet soil

Tree bark

Fog

Water

Torch fire

Nothing glossy.

Nothing futuristic.

---

# Architecture

Everything should feel built by real engineers.

Walls have thickness.

Doors have weight.

Bridges require supports.

Towers have purpose.

Nothing decorative without function.

The fortress has survived hundreds of battles.

Damage tells stories.

Repairs are visible.

Wood replacements.

Stone patches.

Burn marks.

Arrow impacts.

Old siege damage.

---

# Nature

Nature is an ally.

Roots reinforce walls.

Trees protect towers.

Flowers grow between stones.

Moss softens edges.

Nothing should feel abandoned.

The fortress is alive.

---

# Scale

Everything is slightly larger than reality.

Doors

Very large.

Walls

Massive.

Trees

Colossal.

The player should feel small.

---

# Color Palette

Primary

Charcoal

Stone Grey

Moss Green

Oak Brown

Bronze

Warm Amber

Moon Blue

Accent

Soft Gold

Deep Crimson

Never use saturated colors.

Never use pure white.

Never use pure black.

---

# Lighting

Primary light

Moon.

Secondary light

Torches.

The moon defines shape.

The torches define warmth.

The player should immediately understand where warmth exists.

---

# Contrast

Dark world.

Bright points of interest.

Eyes should naturally move toward:

Gate

Torches

Important UI

Never create visual noise.

---

# Fog

Fog creates depth.

Not mystery.

The player must always understand the scene.

Fog should separate layers.

Never hide gameplay.

---

# Composition

Every screen should have one dominant focal point.

Secondary points guide the eye.

Never allow visual clutter.

Empty space is valuable.

---

# Camera

Slow.

Heavy.

Intentional.

Never fast.

Never shaky.

Never arcade-like.

---

# Animation

Everything breathes.

Nothing dances.

Nothing loops obviously.

Movement should be almost subconscious.

---

# References

The feeling—not the art style—should resemble:

Kingdom Come Deliverance

Manor Lords

Frostpunk

Dark Souls

Age of Empires IV

Diablo IV

The Witcher 3

Do not copy any visual assets.

Only study atmosphere and composition.

---

# AI Rules

Every generated asset must answer:

Does this feel handcrafted?

Does this feel believable?

Could this exist?

Does it reinforce the identity?

If any answer is "No",

Reject the asset.
