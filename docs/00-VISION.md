# Bastion Defense

## Vision

Bastion Defense is not simply a Tower Defense game.

It is the story of the final fortress where civilization survives.

The fortress has merged with the surrounding forest over centuries.

Stone, roots, trees and architecture became one living organism.

The main menu is not a menu.

It is the player's first step into the world.

The player should immediately feel:

- solitude
- mystery
- scale
- ancient history
- quiet before war

The scene should feel alive but peaceful.

Never busy.
Never flashy.

Every animation must support immersion.

The player should forget they are looking at HTML.

They should feel they are standing in front of the Bastion.
