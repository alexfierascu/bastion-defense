# The Bastion

Status: Approved

Version: 1.0

Owner: Creative Direction

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Bastion itself.

The Bastion is the central character of the game.

It is more than a castle.

It is the heart of civilization.

Every system in Bastion Defense must reinforce this idea.

---

# Core Identity

The Bastion is the final fortress where humanity survives.

It has withstood centuries of war.

It has never fallen.

It continues to evolve.

Every stone carries history.

Every repaired wall tells a story.

Every root growing through the masonry is a symbol of the alliance between humanity and nature.

---

# Design Principles

The Bastion must always feel:

Massive

Defensible

Lived in

Believable

Engineered

Ancient

Alive

Never:

Empty

Abandoned

Decorative

Overdesigned

Symmetrical for no reason

Fantasy for the sake of fantasy

---

# Architectural Philosophy

Every part of the fortress exists because it serves a purpose.

Nothing is decorative without function.

Every tower sees something.

Every wall protects something.

Every gate controls movement.

Every bridge solves a problem.

The fortress should feel like it was expanded over centuries.

Not designed all at once.

# Districts

The Bastion is divided into functional districts.

Each district has a clear purpose.

## Main Gate

The symbolic entrance.

Largest gate in the fortress.

Massive oak doors.

Iron reinforcement.

Visible siege repairs.

Two gate towers.

Torch-lit day and night.

---

## Gatehouse

Controls access.

Contains:

Guard barracks

Armory

Prison cells

Supply storage

Signal bells

---

## Great Courtyard

Heart of daily life.

Training.

Markets.

Ceremonies.

Supply movement.

Military gatherings.

The courtyard must never feel empty.

---

## Great Tree

The oldest living being inside the Bastion.

Older than the fortress itself.

The castle was built around it.

Its roots reinforce foundations.

Its canopy shades the central courtyard.

It is the visual symbol of Bastion Defense.

Nothing should visually compete with it.

---

## Command Hall

Military headquarters.

Planning.

Maps.

Messenger room.

War council.

Minimal decoration.

Maximum function.

---

## Barracks

Simple.

Practical.

Warm.

Always active.

Equipment is maintained daily.

---

## Smithy

One of the busiest buildings.

Produces:

Weapons

Armor

Tools

Construction hardware

Always contains:

Coal

Wood

Water

Stone

Metal

---

## Workshops

Carpenters.

Stone masons.

Wheelwrights.

Bowyers.

Every profession contributes to maintaining the Bastion.

---

## Granary

Food reserve.

Largest protected storage area.

Heavy security.

Dry construction.

Ventilated.

---

## Water System

The Bastion never depends on rain.

Contains:

Deep wells

Cisterns

Underground channels

Drainage tunnels

Everything follows realistic engineering.

# Defensive Philosophy

The Bastion is designed to delay.

Not simply stop.

Every defensive layer buys time.

Outer terrain.

Bridge.

Gate.

Courtyard.

Inner walls.

Upper Bastion.

Final Keep.

---

# Verticality

Elevation is a strategic advantage.

The Bastion is built upward.

Every important location overlooks another.

Players should constantly feel protected from above.

# Construction Language

Every repair remains visible.

New stone is brighter.

Old stone is darker.

Wood replacements never perfectly match.

Metal rusts gradually.

Moss grows where moisture remains.

The fortress continuously evolves.

---

# Wear and History

Damage tells stories.

Examples:

Arrow impacts.

Collapsed battlements.

Repaired towers.

Burn marks.

Replacement beams.

Weather erosion.

Nothing should appear factory new.

---

# Roads

Roads always have destinations.

No decorative paths.

Main roads:

Main Gate → Courtyard

Courtyard → Command Hall

Courtyard → Smithy

Courtyard → Barracks

Courtyard → Workshops

Secondary roads:

Watch towers

Storage

Gardens

Training yard

Every path serves logistics.

---

# Vegetation

Nature exists with intention.

Roots strengthen walls.

Flowers appear in peaceful areas.

Moss grows in damp locations.

Grass follows sunlight.

Trees are maintained.

Nothing feels abandoned.

# Future Expansion

The Bastion should visibly evolve during the campaign.

Examples:

Additional banners.

Repaired walls.

Expanded workshops.

New defensive towers.

Improved roads.

Seasonal decorations.

The player should feel responsible for its growth.

---

# AI Rules

When generating any new structure:

Ask:

Does it have a purpose?

Can someone realistically build it?

Does it improve defense?

Would it survive centuries?

Does it visually belong to the Bastion?

If any answer is "No", reject the design.
