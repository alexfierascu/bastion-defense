# World Overview

Status: Approved

Version: 1.0

Owner: Creative Direction

---

# Purpose

This document defines the world of Bastion Defense.

Every visual, gameplay, audio, UI and technical decision must reinforce the world described here.

If another document contradicts this one, this document takes precedence until officially updated.

---

# Vision

Bastion Defense takes place at the final stronghold of civilization.

The Bastion is not simply a castle.

It is a living organism.

Centuries ago, mankind attempted to conquer nature.

Instead, nature chose to protect mankind.

Stone and roots became one.

Walls and forests became inseparable.

The Bastion is alive.

The player is not defending a kingdom.

The player is defending the final place where humanity and nature coexist.

---

# Design Philosophy

The world must always communicate:

- permanence
- resilience
- craftsmanship
- hope
- preparation
- history

The world must never communicate:

- apocalypse
- horror
- chaos
- corruption
- decay
- cartoon fantasy

This is not the end of civilization.

It is its strongest remaining sanctuary.

---

# World Pillars

## The Bastion

Everything revolves around the Bastion.

The fortress is the main character.

Everything exists because of it.

Every road leads to it.

Every enemy seeks it.

Every ally protects it.

---

## Living Nature

Nature is intelligent.

It reinforces.

It protects.

It remembers.

Roots strengthen foundations.

Trees shield towers from wind.

Flowers grow after battles.

Moss heals damaged stone.

Nothing is random.

---

## Believable Engineering

Every structure must appear functional.

Every bridge has support.

Every tower serves a military purpose.

Every wall has believable thickness.

Every gate has reinforcement.

Decoration never replaces function.

---

## History

The world feels old.

Not abandoned.

Old roads.

Old walls.

Old trees.

Old victories.

History is visible everywhere.

Players should discover stories by observing the environment.

---

# Geographic Overview

The known world consists of several large regions.

The player only experiences a small part of it.

The Bastion sits at the center of a fertile valley surrounded by ancient forests and mountain ranges.

Natural geography creates the first line of defense.

```
           Mountains

    Forest           Forest

          River

      The Bastion

    Farmland

Southern Trade Road
```

The Bastion was intentionally constructed where terrain naturally limits enemy movement.

# Climate

Temperate.

Four seasons.

Every season affects the appearance of the world.

Spring

Fresh vegetation.

Bright greens.

Blooming flowers.

Morning mist.

Summer

Dense forests.

Long evenings.

Fireflies.

Warm torchlight.

Autumn

Orange leaves.

Heavy fog.

Wet ground.

Harvest.

Winter

Snow.

Frozen rivers.

Bare trees.

Long nights.

The game should support seasonal visual variations without changing gameplay readability.

---

# Color Identity

The world is grounded.

Colors should always appear weathered.

Primary palette:

Stone Grey

Moss Green

Oak Brown

Moon Blue

Torch Amber

Secondary palette:

Forest Green

Bronze

Leather Brown

Muted Crimson

Never use highly saturated colors.

Never use synthetic-looking materials.

---

# Civilization

Human settlements are rare.

Every village exists because the Bastion protects it.

Villages are practical.

Woodcutters.

Farmers.

Blacksmiths.

Stone masons.

Carpenters.

Hunters.

Everything produced eventually supports the Bastion.

---

# Nature

Nature is never hostile without reason.

Animals behave naturally.

Birds migrate.

Foxes hunt.

Deer avoid conflict.

Owls hunt at night.

The forest feels alive because it follows believable ecological rules.

---

# Technology

Technology resembles the late medieval period.

No firearms.

No steam engines.

No industrial machinery.

Engineering is advanced.

Materials are traditional.

Knowledge is accumulated through generations.

---

# Magic

Magic is subtle.

The player never throws fireballs.

Nature itself contains ancient energy.

Roots grow faster.

Trees heal.

Seeds respond to seasons.

The Bastion slowly repairs itself through its bond with the forest.

Magic should always feel believable.

Never flashy.

---

# Threat

The world is dangerous beyond the forests.

Most people never travel far.

Roads require patrols.

Supply caravans require protection.

The Bastion exists because danger never disappeared.

The details of enemy factions are defined in FACTIONS.md.

---

# Daily Life

Despite constant danger, life continues.

Children learn trades.

Blacksmiths work.

Farmers harvest.

Carpenters repair walls.

Guards rotate shifts.

Messengers travel roads.

Life exists because someone stands watch.

---

# Environmental Storytelling

Every environment should tell a story without dialogue.

Examples:

A repaired wall.

Arrow impacts.

A broken cart.

Fresh footprints.

A shrine beside a road.

An abandoned campfire.

An old siege ladder.

A young tree growing through ancient stone.

Players should understand history through observation.

---

# Audio Identity

Silence is valuable.

The world should never feel empty.

Ambient sounds include:

Wind.

Leaves.

Birds.

Water.

Distant bells.

Wood creaks.

Torch fire.

Footsteps.

Owls.

No constant orchestral music.

Music supports emotional moments rather than filling silence.

---

# Gameplay Relationship

The world supports gameplay.

Gameplay never breaks the world.

New towers must appear constructed by Bastion engineers.

Roads influence enemy paths.

Terrain explains defensive choices.

Buildings always have believable access points.

The environment should justify every mechanic.

---

# AI Rules

When generating assets:

Ask:

Would this realistically exist?

Does it reinforce the Bastion's identity?

Does it feel handcrafted?

Would a medieval engineer approve its construction?

Does nature cooperate with it?

If any answer is "No", reject the asset.
