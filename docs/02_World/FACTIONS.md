# Factions

Status: Approved

Version: 1.0

Owner: Creative Direction

---

# Purpose

This document defines every major faction that exists within the world of Bastion Defense.

The goal is not to create dozens of fantasy races.

The goal is to create believable civilizations with clear motivations.

Every faction should have:

- A purpose
- A visual identity
- A military doctrine
- A culture
- A relationship with the Bastion

---

# Design Philosophy

Every faction must answer one question:

"What are they willing to sacrifice to survive?"

Their answer defines everything else.

Architecture.

Weapons.

Armor.

Music.

Behavior.

Politics.

Technology.

---

# Current Factions

Version 1.0 intentionally defines only three primary factions.

Future factions may be added only if they expand gameplay.

Never add factions simply for variety.

# The Bastion

Role

The defenders.

Identity

Civilized.

Disciplined.

Patient.

Resilient.

Their objective is not conquest.

Their objective is survival.

---

## Military Doctrine

Defend.

Delay.

Outlast.

Counterattack only when necessary.

---

## Strengths

Exceptional engineering.

Defensive warfare.

Logistics.

Archery.

Fortifications.

Knowledge.

---

## Weaknesses

Limited population.

Limited resources.

Expansion is slow.

Every loss matters.

---

## Visual Language

Stone.

Oak.

Bronze.

Canvas.

Leather.

Muted colors.

Weathered equipment.

Visible repairs.

Nothing ornamental.

---

## Symbols

The Great Tree.

The Bastion Gate.

The Root.

The Shield.

The Torch.

# The Ashen Horde

Role

Primary enemy faction.

---

## Philosophy

The Horde does not seek wealth.

The Horde does not seek territory.

The Horde believes civilization weakens humanity.

Only constant struggle creates strength.

They attack because peace is considered corruption.

---

## Culture

Nomadic.

Brutal.

Practical.

Strength determines leadership.

Failure removes authority.

---

## Military Doctrine

Overwhelm.

Constant pressure.

Large numbers.

Siege.

Attrition.

---

## Strengths

Large armies.

Fast recruitment.

Aggressive tactics.

Fearless assaults.

---

## Weaknesses

Poor logistics.

Weak engineering.

Low discipline.

Internal conflict.

---

## Visual Language

Dark iron.

Charred leather.

Ash-covered wood.

Black banners.

Rope.

Riveted armor.

Scars.

Burn marks.

No polished equipment.

---

## Weapons

Axes.

Heavy clubs.

Spears.

Siege towers.

Battering rams.

Primitive catapults.

---

## Architecture

Temporary.

Wood.

Earthworks.

Field camps.

Watch fires.

Nothing permanent.

---

## Audio

War drums.

Horn blasts.

Heavy footsteps.

Chains.

Roaring fires.

# The Wardens

Role

Neutral faction.

---

## Philosophy

The Wardens protect the balance between civilization and nature.

Neither side may dominate.

If the Bastion forgets its responsibility toward the forest, the Wardens intervene.

---

## Culture

Quiet.

Patient.

Observant.

Knowledge passed orally.

Few written records.

---

## Military Doctrine

Precision.

Ambush.

Reconnaissance.

Avoid unnecessary battles.

---

## Strengths

Tracking.

Archery.

Camouflage.

Forest survival.

Intelligence gathering.

---

## Weaknesses

Small population.

Little heavy equipment.

Limited siege capability.

---

## Visual Language

Green cloth.

Natural leather.

Wood.

Bronze.

Simple tools.

Minimal armor.

Practical clothing.

---

## Architecture

Forest lodges.

Watch posts.

Hidden bridges.

Tree platforms.

Stone circles.

Structures disappear into the landscape.

---

## Audio

Bird calls.

Wind chimes.

Wood creaks.

Flowing water.

Soft footsteps.

# Design Rules

Every faction must be recognizable by silhouette alone.

Remove color.

Remove textures.

The player should still identify the faction.

Silhouette matters more than detail.

---

# Equipment Rules

Equipment reflects culture.

The Bastion repairs equipment.

The Horde replaces equipment.

The Wardens maintain equipment.

Every scratch has meaning.

Every repair tells a story.

---

# Expansion Rules

Future factions must introduce:

A new philosophy.

A new combat style.

A new architectural language.

A new visual identity.

A new gameplay opportunity.

If not, do not create the faction.

---

# AI Rules

Never create:

Generic fantasy kingdoms.

"Evil because evil."

Skulls everywhere.

Oversized armor.

Impractical weapons.

Unrealistic architecture.

Instead create:

Believable societies.

Purpose-driven equipment.

Distinct silhouettes.

Logical military organization.

Consistent materials.

Recognizable identity.

# Success Criteria

A player should identify every faction within three seconds using only:

Architecture.

Armor.

Weapons.

Silhouette.

Movement.

Materials.

Color palette.

No dialogue should be required to understand who they are.
