# Resource Economy

Status: Approved

Version: 1.0

Owner: Game Design

---

# Purpose

This document defines every resource used in Bastion Defense and the philosophy behind the game's economy.

The economy should constantly force interesting decisions.

There should never be enough resources to build everything.

---

# Philosophy

Resources represent choices.

Not rewards.

Every resource spent should mean another opportunity has been sacrificed.

---

# Design Goals

The economy should:

Reward planning.

Reward efficiency.

Reward adaptation.

Encourage specialization.

Prevent dominant strategies.

Remain understandable.

---

# Core Resources

## Gold

Primary construction resource.

Used for:

- Building towers
- Upgrading towers
- Repairs
- Emergency defenses

Gold is earned by defeating enemies and completing objectives.

---

## Wood

Construction material.

Used for:

- Basic towers
- Walls
- Barricades
- Bridges
- Temporary defenses

Wood is abundant early.

Less valuable late.

---

## Stone

Infrastructure resource.

Used for:

- Permanent defenses
- Walls
- Keeps
- Gate upgrades

Stone is slower to obtain.

More valuable than wood.

---

## Iron

Military resource.

Used for:

- Advanced towers
- Ballistas
- Cannons
- Elite upgrades

Iron should always feel valuable.

---

## Food

Supports defenders.

Used for:

- Recruiting units
- Maintaining garrisons
- Hero recovery

Food creates long-term planning.

---

## Influence

Represents leadership.

Used for:

- Hero abilities
- Emergency commands
- Special structures
- Village support

Influence is intentionally limited.

---

# Economy Rules

Resources should never become meaningless.

If players stop caring about a resource,

the economy has failed.

---

# Spending Priorities

Every purchase competes against another.

Examples:

Tower

or

Upgrade

Repair

or

New defense

Economy

or

Military

Every decision should have consequences.

---

# Income

Income comes from:

Enemy defeats.

Wave completion.

Objectives.

Exploration.

Special buildings.

Never from passive inflation alone.

---

# Scarcity

Scarcity creates strategy.

Abundance removes decisions.

Resources should become more valuable,

not simply more numerous.

---

# Comeback Mechanics

Recovering should be possible.

Recovering should never be free.

Players should be rewarded for surviving difficult situations.

---

# Snowball Prevention

Winning should create options.

Not inevitability.

Leading players should gain flexibility,

not unstoppable power.

---

# UI

Players should always know:

Current resources.

Income sources.

Upcoming costs.

Resource shortages.

Future expenses.

No hidden information.

---

# AI Rules

When designing a new mechanic ask:

Which resource does it consume?

Which decision does it create?

Does it compete with existing options?

Can players understand its value?

If not,

redesign it.
