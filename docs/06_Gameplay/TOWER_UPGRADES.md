# Tower Upgrades

Status: Approved

Version: 1.0

Owner: Game Design

---

# Purpose

This document defines how towers evolve throughout a match.

Upgrades should change strategy.

They should never simply increase statistics.

Players should feel they are specializing a tower instead of making it "Level 2".

---

# Philosophy

Every upgrade asks:

"What role do you want this tower to perform?"

Not:

"How much stronger should it become?"

---

# Design Goals

Tower upgrades should:

Create meaningful choices.

Encourage specialization.

Support multiple strategies.

Reward planning.

Avoid obvious upgrade paths.

---

# Upgrade Structure

Every tower follows:

Tier I

↓

Tier II

↓

Tier III

↓

Tier IV

↓

Ultimate Specialization

Each tier unlocks a new gameplay decision.

---

# Upgrade Categories

Every tower upgrades through one or more categories.

Damage

Range

Utility

Area Control

Support

Economy

Survivability

Precision

Targeting

No tower should excel in every category.

---

# Horizontal Progression

Preferred.

Example:

Arrow Tower

↓

Longbow Specialist

or

Rapid Fire Archer

Both are equally viable.

They solve different problems.

---

# Vertical Progression

Allowed only when necessary.

Example

Ballista

↓

Improved Ballista

↓

Heavy Ballista

↓

Siege Ballista

Even here, every tier should unlock new mechanics.

---

---

# Upgrade Rules

Every upgrade must:

Introduce a new decision.

Change gameplay.

Remain visually distinct.

Be immediately understandable.

Support replayability.

---

# Visual Progression

Players should identify upgrade level without opening menus.

Methods

Architecture.

Materials.

Flags.

Banners.

Roof shape.

Weapon size.

Animation.

Lighting.

Silhouette.

---

# Upgrade Costs

Each tier becomes more expensive.

Cost growth should be meaningful.

Expensive upgrades should compete with:

Building another tower.

Repairing defenses.

Research.

Hero investment.

---

# Selling

Players may sell towers.

Refund

70% default.

Refund percentage may increase through research.

Selling should encourage adaptation.

Not abuse.

---

# Respecialization

Late-game technology may allow:

Changing specialization.

Keeping tower level.

Paying a resource cost.

Respecialization rewards adaptation.

---

Longbow

Scout Tower

+

Banner Tower

+

---

# AI Rules

Before creating an upgrade ask:

Does it change gameplay?

Would experienced players debate between choices?

Does it support a unique strategy?

Can it be identified visually?

If any answer is "No",

redesign it.

# Success Criteria

Players should be excited every time a tower upgrades.

The question should never be:

"Do I upgrade?"

It should always be:

"Which specialization do I choose?"
